Week 8: CNC

Me and Sophia worked together to make a mold of a gummy bear in fusion! The longest part was honestly just making our fusion file look like a bear, but we eventually got it looking somewhat familiar.

Then, we exported it into an stl file, and cut it out on the CNC machine. We had to cut both parts separately, because we exported the two halves of the mold as separate files. So, once we had cut them out, we used silicone to create our very own gummy bear mold.

Unfortuantely, me and Sofia did not have the chance to make the actual mold from out wax cutout because we were working on our MVPs. Next time we go to the lab, we plan on making our mold and casting out bear out of gelatin.
